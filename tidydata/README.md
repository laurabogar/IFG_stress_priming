# tidydata/

Per-plant chlorophyll fluorescence and stomatal conductance for the IFG stress-priming experiment, measured with LI-COR LI-600 / LI-600N porometer/fluorometers. These files are generated by `make_tidy_licor_data.R`, so don't edit them by hand.

Each plant was measured about 3 times. Per-plant values are means of the measurements that passed QC (see below). `plantID` is the 3-digit plant number, which is the key for joining these files to RNA-seq sample metadata.

## Files

| File | Species | Date | Light state | What's in it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `populus_fvfm_by_plant.csv` | Populus | 15 Oct 2024 | dark-adapted | Fv/Fm |
| `populus_gsw_dark_by_plant.csv` | Populus | 15 Oct 2024 | dark-adapted | stomatal conductance |
| `populus_gsw_light_by_plant.csv` | Populus | 18 Oct 2024 | ambient light | stomatal conductance |
| `pseudotsuga_fvfm_by_plant.csv` | Douglas-fir | 4 Nov 2024 | dark-adapted | Fv/Fm |
| `li600_measurements_qc.csv` | both | all | both | every measurement, with QC pass/fail columns |

There is no Douglas-fir gsw file, because no attempt was made to measure gsw properly on needles.

The two Populus gsw files measure different things. Stomata partly close in the dark, so dark gsw is much lower (median of plant means about 0.017 vs 0.038 mol m⁻² s⁻¹). Light gsw also responds to the light level at the time, which varied from about 80 to 550 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹; see `mean_Qamb`. For plants that have both, the plant means correlate at r ≈ 0.6.

## Columns

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `plantID` | 3-digit plant ID |
| `Species` | `Poplar` or `Douglas fir` |
| `soil` / `soil_code` | Soil inoculum: source species × live or sterilized (DE, DFS, PE, PS) |
| `ABA` / `ABA_tape_color` | ABA treatment: `3 wks` (O), `2 wks` (Y), `0 wks` (G), `None` (B) |
| `fvfm_mean`, `fvfm_sd` | Fv/Fm (maximum PSII quantum efficiency, unitless) |
| `n_low_signal` | Number of averaged Fv/Fm readings with a weak fluorescence signal (Fm < 100) |
| `gsw_mean`, `gsw_sd` | Stomatal conductance to water vapor (mol m⁻² s⁻¹) |
| `mean_Qamb`, `mean_Tleaf`, `mean_VPDleaf`, `mean_leak_pct` | Measurement conditions for gsw (µmol m⁻² s⁻¹, °C, kPa, %) |
| `n_measurements` | Number of readings averaged |
| `measurement_date`, `first_time`, `instrument` | Provenance; values are separated by `;` if there was more than one |

## QC rules

- **Fv/Fm:** a reading is dropped if the person measuring flagged it, or if Fv/Fm is outside (0, 0.85]. Values above about 0.85 aren't physiologically plausible. Here they come from readings with Fo near zero.
- **gsw:** a reading is dropped if it was flagged, if the chamber leak was over 10%, or if it was taken right after a bad humidity-sensor match. The LI-600 matches its two humidity sensors about every 10 minutes. After four of those matches (three on 15 Oct, one on 18 Oct), every reading until the next match was shifted by a near-constant amount. For example, all the 15 Oct readings in one window were about −0.24, and all in another were about +0.21.
- **Negative gsw values are kept.** True gsw here is small, so good readings scatter on both sides of zero. Dropping the negatives would bias the means upward.

## Corrections and known data notes

- **Plant 031:** the tape color was blank in the treatment key. It is set to no ABA, from the RNA metadata.
- **Plant 122:** its 15 Oct readings were flagged, but the flags were judged to be a mistake. These were the first readings of the session, and the values are normal.
- **Plant 007 (Douglas-fir):** excluded, because its ABA treatment isn't recorded.
- **Plant 026:** has no LI-600 readings.
- **Dark adaptation:** whole plants were dark-adapted together for at least 30 minutes in a closed, dark garage, then measured.
- **Douglas-fir measurement method:** Douglas-fir needles are too small to fill the LI-600N aperture. Each reading was taken on a bunch of needles clamped together, with no attempt at a seal, so that as much chlorophyll as possible was under the sensor. As a result:
  - The amount of tissue under the sensor varied, so the signal strength (Fm) varied.
  - Readings from the same plant differ about twice as much as for Populus (median within-plant SD 0.049 vs 0.023).
  - The absolute Fv/Fm values may be biased by needles overlapping and shading one another, so don't compare them to published conifer values.
  - Comparisons between treatments look sound. Fm doesn't differ by soil or ABA treatment, and adding Fm as a covariate changes the treatment effects by ≤ 0.003.
  - `n_low_signal` shows how many weak readings (Fm < 100) went into each plant's mean.
- **Douglas-fir instruments:** seven plants were measured on both instruments, and their means pool both.
